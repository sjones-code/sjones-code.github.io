Drone payload release: Better Place Drones

Team entry in the XPRIZE autonomous firefighting competition

I designed a CO2-driven blowdown system: a CO2 canister pressurizes a water tank to push a fire-suppression stream out of an autonomous drone.

Design and integration

  • Constraint: strict size and weight limits, since every gram cuts flight time.
  • Method: iterate CAD in SolidWorks, print, test, revise.
  • Coordinated mounting and mass with five subteams from drawings to proof of concept.

Blowdown validation

Before adding the valve and canister, we validated the tank assembly empty: structure, fit, and plumbing paths.

  • Blowdown means stored gas pressure alone drives the water out, with no pump or motor.
  • Fewer moving parts means lower mass and fewer failure points on a drone.
